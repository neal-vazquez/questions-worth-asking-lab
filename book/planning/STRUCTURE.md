# Book structure

The anthology keeps its established four-part structure. This consolidates durable decisions from the former Book Bible and outline. Detailed unpublished chapter hypotheses remain in the protected archive pending private GitHub custody.

| Part | Question | Progression |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | How should we live? | Life, meaning, ambition, identity, and practice |
| II | How should we treat one another? | Ethics, responsibility, relationships, systems, and care |
| III | What should we believe? | Knowledge, evidence, worldviews, religion, and disagreement |
| IV | What are we becoming? | Technology, agency, cognition, governance, and human change |

The movement runs from individual life through moral relationships and belief to technological and social transformation. Place pieces by their dominant question; do not duplicate them merely because they cross disciplines.

The old planning documents proposed 16 trade-book chapters and possible consolidation to 10–14. Those are historical hypotheses, not a mandate to dismantle the 63-piece reader or a current word-count commitment. Test future trade-book structure against verified author material.

Front and back matter follow [DECISIONS.md](../DECISIONS.md). Keep the reader, trade-book proposal, and source prose distinguishable.
